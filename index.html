How it works

  1. Send the manuscript. Email the file, the target journal, and your deadline. I read the abstract and first pages and reply with a fixed quote within one business day.
  2. Confirm and pay the deposit. Card, bank transfer, and Alipay are accepted. Formal invoices are provided for institutional reimbursement.
  3. Receive the edit. A tracked-changes file, a clean copy, and, for substantive edits, the editorial memo. Ask any follow-up questions within two weeks.

I edit; I do not write. Authorship, data, and conclusions remain yours, and I never reuse or share manuscripts. Every edit is made by me, and manuscripts are never entered into AI tools.

About

Alluvial Editing is run by David Ure, PhD, who edits every manuscript personally. I am a postdoctoral fellow at the University of Windsor in Ontario, Canada. My research covers nutrient cycling, aqueous geochemistry, water quality, and stable isotope methods in agricultural and Great Lakes systems. I have published first-author papers in ACS Agricultural Science & Technology, the Journal of Environmental Management, and Rapid Communications in Mass Spectrometry, and I have served as a peer reviewer for the Journal of Chemical Technology and Biotechnology.

Before my doctorate, I taught English as a second language in Seoul for three years and hold a TESOL certificate (London Language Institute, 2013). Editing for researchers writing in a second language draws on both halves of that background.

Since 2022, I have edited approximately 1,400 manuscripts for authors publishing in English-language journals, including substantive edits with direct author correspondence. Most of that work has been for researchers writing in English as a second language, in soil science, plant physiology, environmental chemistry, and environmental engineering.

What that means for you: I know which claims reviewers in these fields will challenge, which methods details journals now require, and how to make a good study read like one.

Fields I edit: soil biogeochemistry, water quality, nutrient cycling, plant physiology, environmental chemistry, environmental and agricultural engineering, and adjacent molecular biology.

Outside these fields, I will review and either accept or refer elsewhere if necessary.
