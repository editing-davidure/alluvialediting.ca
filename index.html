Scientific and Language Editing for Agricultural and Environmental Science

Alluvial Sciences provides scientific and language editing for English-language manuscripts and theses. All edits are performed by a published PhD Scientist (David Ure PhD). Each edit addresses correctness, grammar, and style, as well as the methods and data presentation, with emphasis on phrasing that often causes reviewers to misread ESL authors.

Editor’s comment: Please note, “proves” overstates the evidence provided by a correlation, which reviewers may raise in review. Presenting the conclusion as a supported inference, and stating that nitrate isotopic data are required to exclude manure or septic sources, addresses this objection.

Process

  1. Submit the manuscript. Send the manuscript file, target journal (if known), and required deadline by email (david@alluvialsciences.ca). The editor reviews the abstract and opening pages and provides a fixed quote within one business day.
  2. Confirm and pay the deposit. Payment is accepted by credit card or bank transfer. Formal invoices are provided for institutional payment or reimbursement.
  3. Receive the edited manuscript. Each delivery includes a tracked-changes file, a clean copy, an editing certificate, and the balance invoice. Scientific Editing includes an editorial memo, and Submission Preparation includes a journal shortlist, cover letter, and other agreed upon materials. Follow-up questions may be sent within 14 days of delivery.

Alluvial Sciences edits manuscripts rather than author them. Authorship, data, and conclusions remain the author’s, and manuscripts are never shared or distributed. All edits are performed by David Ure, PhD. Manuscripts are never entered into LLM or AI models.

About

Alluvial Sciences is operated by David Ure, PhD (Chemistry and Biochemistry), who edits all manuscripts. Dr. Ure's research addresses nutrient cycling, aqueous geochemistry, water quality, and stable isotope methods in agricultural and Great Lakes systems. He has published first-author papers in ACS Agricultural Science & Technology, the Journal of Environmental Management, Rapid Communications in Mass Spectrometry, and the Journal of Chemical Technology and Biotechnology.

Since 2022, Dr. Ure has edited approximately 1,400 manuscripts for authors publishing in English-language journals, including scientific edits involving direct correspondence with authors. Most of this work has been in agricultural and environmental science.

Alluvial Sciences accepts manuscripts in soil biogeochemistry, water quality, nutrient cycling, plant physiology, environmental chemistry, environmental and agricultural engineering, and related areas of molecular biology.

Manuscripts in other fields are assessed individually and are either accepted or referred elsewhere.
